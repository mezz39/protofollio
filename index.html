LMS Assignments and Submissions Workflow

Business analysis case study for a cohort-based learning platform for a Business Analysis bootcamp. A case scenario of about 800 learners was the baseline for capacity, requirements and flow design. It is a practice project, not a deployed system.

The problem. Assignment types, submissions, mentor review and grading were handled by hand across several tools and messages. This made status hard to see, feedback slow, and results hard to track.

My role. I analyzed the current process, modeled it in BPMN, designed the future process, and wrote the user stories, acceptance criteria, test cases and traceability matrix. I also logged every open question and recorded each decision.

Key decisions

Questions I raised during analysis, how they were resolved, and what changed in the requirements.

OQ-03. Draft versus final

Question: Is mentor approval of a draft mandatory before final submission?

Decision: Yes.

Effect on the requirements: Draft approval is a mandatory gate before final submission. Added a proposed AC/TC for blocking final submission.

OQ-04. Acceptance and grade

Question: Should Accepted and Graded be separate statuses, or should the grade be a field on an accepted assignment?

Decision: Two separate statuses.

Effect on the requirements: Added AC/TC for the Graded status.

OQ-06. Feedback visibility

Question: Can learners see draft feedback immediately? When is the final grade released?

Decision: Draft feedback and the grade are released once the mentor submits, all together, not sequentially.

Effect on the requirements: Nothing is visible until the mentor submits. At the revision stage no grade is delivered (OQ-15).

OQ-07. Notifications

Question: Which channels are used (in-app, email, or both), and should notifications link directly to the assignment or feedback?

Decision: Email. A reminder only, with no direct link, but with the link to the website to log in.

Effect on the requirements: Email reminder with LMS login link; no direct deep links.

OQ-09. Status vocabulary

Question: Should the LMS use only the five statuses in the notes, or add Under Review and Graded?

Decision: Add them.

Effect on the requirements: Seven statuses. Under Review trigger resolved in OQ-14.

OQ-12. Link validation

Question: What URL validation and file/folder link rules apply to the Google Drive link?

Decision: This was a manual as-is step; the automated workflow will not need it.

Effect on the requirements: No URL or file-naming validation required.

One question, deadline rules (OQ-08), is in scope but only partly defined, so US-020 is flagged as needing confirmation.
